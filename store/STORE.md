## English (U.S.)

**Promotional text (170)**

Seven rooms, seventy levels and one very innocent-looking cat. Knock it all down, then be asleep in your bed before anyone walks in.

**Description (4000)**

The family is out. The house is yours.

You are a cat with a simple plan: knock everything off every shelf, ride the ceiling fan, pull down the curtains, and be curled up asleep before anyone opens the door.

HOW IT PLAYS
• Drag back and let go to launch your cat like a slingshot. That is the whole control scheme.
• Everything wobbles, falls, rolls and smashes. Chain hits for combos and fill the Zoomies meter for double points.
• Hear footsteps? Land and freeze. If nobody sees you move, nobody can prove a thing.
• When the key turns in the lock, race back to your bed and look innocent.

SEVEN ROOMS, SEVENTY LEVELS
• Living room: Christmas tree, ceiling fan and curtains.
• Kitchen: plates, pans, flour and a tablecloth trick.
• Office: spinning chair, printer, cables and a robot vacuum.
• Bathroom: bath foam, rubber ducks and a flooded floor.
• Bedroom: bouncy mattress, feather pillows and a very loud alarm clock.
• Attic: a tower of boxes, a swinging light bulb, a ghost and a bat.
• Garden: birds on the fence, a tyre swing, a trampoline and a sprinkler.

Every room has its own tricks and ten levels that get harder as you go. Earn up to three stars on each one.

REASONS TO COME BACK
• Collect fish and spend them on upgrades: springier jumps, a heavier paw, sharper ears.
• Find hidden trophies and fill your album.
• A daily bowl that gets fuller every day you return.
• Free play in every room once you know your way around.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Plays offline. Perfect for airports, queues and waiting rooms.
• No ads. No in-app purchases. No accounts. No data collected.
• Short rounds of about 45 seconds.
• English and Polish.

No cats, grandmothers or vases were harmed.
